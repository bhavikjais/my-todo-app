Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

In short: we store only what the app needs to work. Your tasks are private unless you choose to share one by link. We don’t sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking. You can delete your account and data at any time from inside the app.

Who we are

Tasks (“the app”) is a to-do list app. If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at bhavikjais12@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and name. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your Google profile name and photo. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
  • Profile: the name and optional role you enter, and the tasks you bookmark.
  • Your content: tasks, with titles, projects, descriptions, priorities, due dates, subtasks and images you attach, plus an activity log of changes (for example “Marked as completed”, with the name of the person who made the change).

We do not collect your location, contacts, device identifiers or advertising IDs. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your tasks in sync across your devices.
  • To show shared tasks, and changes to them, to the people you share them with.

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Sharing

  • Private tasks (the default) can be seen only by you.
  • Shared-via-link tasks can be opened by anyone who signs in with the link. People who add the task can see it, along with its subtasks, attachments and activity log, and they can update its progress. They also see your name, and you see theirs. You can make a task private again at any time, which removes everyone else’s access immediately.
  • Service provider: data is stored and processed by Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google LLC under its own privacy and security terms. Data is encrypted in transit.

We will only disclose data beyond this if the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete a task, it is removed. When you delete your account, your data is deleted as described below.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: open the menu (☰) → Delete account, then confirm. This immediately and permanently deletes:

  • your sign-in account and profile;
  • every task you created, including subtasks and attachments, for everyone it was shared with;
  • attachments you added to other people’s tasks, and your membership of those tasks. In their activity logs, your name is replaced with “Deleted user”.

Without the app: email bhavikjais12@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will delete your account and data within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can view and edit your data in the app at any time, and delete it as described above. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights, such as access, correction, portability or objection. Contact us to exercise them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If the changes are significant, we will also let you know in the app.
